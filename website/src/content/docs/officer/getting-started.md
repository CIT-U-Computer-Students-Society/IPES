Welcome to IPES! As a student officer or committee member, you will use IPES to complete peer evaluations for your fellow officers and maintain your portfolio of verified organizational accomplishments.

---

## Logging In to the Portal

To access your evaluation assignments, navigate to the IPES application portal (e.g., `http://localhost:5173/login` or your organization's deployment URL).

<img src="/images/annotated/officer/login-and-select-org.svg" alt="Sign In and Organization Selection Annotated Interface" class="guide-screenshot" />

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. <span class="step-badge">1</span> **Enter your institutional email & password**: Provide your registered university email and secure credentials.
2. <span class="step-badge">2</span> **Click "Sign In"**: The system verifies your credentials and creates an encrypted, HttpOnly session.

:::note[Account Activation]
If your student organization hasn't invited your account yet, or you need your password reset, contact your organization's IPES Administrator.
:::

---

## Selecting Your Organization

IPES supports multi-tenancy. If you are an active member of multiple student organizations (for example, both the Supreme Student Government and the Computer Students Society), you will be prompted to select your active organization after logging in:

1. <span class="step-badge-amber step-badge">3</span> **Click on the target organization card**: Choose the organization you wish to work in. All evaluations, accomplishment records, and dashboards will immediately scope to this organization.

:::tip[Switching Organizations]
You can switch organizations at any time without logging out by clicking the organization dropdown in the top navigation bar.
:::

---

## Navigating the Interface & Setting Preferences

Once an organization is selected, you will be taken to your **Officer Dashboard**.

- **Theme Toggle**: Located at the top right of the navigation header. Click the Sun/Moon icon to switch between Light Mode and Dark Mode. IPES automatically remembers your theme preference across sessions.
- **Role Switcher**: If your membership grants administrative privileges in addition to officer duties, you will see a toggle in the top bar to switch between the **Officer View** and **Admin View**.
- **Sign Out**: Click your user avatar in the top right corner and select **Log Out** to securely terminate your session.

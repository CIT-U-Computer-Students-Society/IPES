Once an evaluation cycle has concluded and administrators have officially **Released** the results, your individual performance summary becomes available under **Results** (`/member/results`).

---

## The Performance Scorecard

<img src="/images/annotated/officer/results-scorecard.svg" alt="Performance Results Scorecard Annotated Interface" class="guide-screenshot" />

### Reading Your Visual Metrics

1. <span class="step-badge-amber step-badge">1</span> **Composite Score & Rating**:
   - The weighted average score across all evaluators and categories (e.g., 4.62 / 5.00).
   - Shows your percentile position relative to peers across the organization (e.g., Top 10%).

2. <span class="step-badge-navy step-badge">2</span> **Competency Category Averages**:
   - Multi-axis breakdown depicting relative strengths across dimensions (Leadership, Execution, Teamwork, Communication).

3. <span class="step-badge">3</span> **Anonymized Peer Remarks**:
   - Qualitative comments written by peers who evaluated you.
   - Evaluator names, timestamps, and IPs are decoupled at the database level to protect evaluator confidentiality while offering constructive guidance.

:::note[Cycle Availability]
Results are visible only after an administrator transitions the evaluation cycle from **Closed** to **Released**.
:::

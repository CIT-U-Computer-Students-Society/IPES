The **Officer Dashboard** (`/member/dashboard`) is your mission control center in IPES. When an evaluation cycle is active, this page outlines your pending peer assignments, upcoming submission deadlines, and portfolio accomplishment statuses.

---

## Dashboard Overview

<img src="/images/annotated/officer/dashboard-overview.svg" alt="Officer Dashboard Annotated Interface" class="guide-screenshot" />

### Key Metrics Explained

1. <span class="step-badge">1</span> **Pending Evaluations**: The number of peers assigned to you whose evaluation forms are either *Not Started* or currently *In Progress*.
2. <span class="step-badge">2</span> **Completed Evaluations**: The count of evaluations you have finalized and submitted for the active cycle.
3. <span class="step-badge-amber step-badge">2</span> **Verified Accomplishments**: The total number of accomplishments in your portfolio that have been reviewed and approved by an administrator.
4. **Cycle Deadline**: The remaining days before the active evaluation window closes. Submissions lock automatically once the deadline expires.

---

## Action Items in Your Task Queue

5. <span class="step-badge">3</span> **Active Evaluation Queue**:
   - Each card lists an evaluatee's name, their committee unit, and their position.
   - Click **Start Form** to open a new evaluation.
   - Click **Continue** to resume an evaluation you previously drafted and saved.
   - Once all assigned evaluations reach *Completed* status, your queue displays a green checkmark indicating 100% completion.

:::note[Confidentiality Assurance]
Evaluation assignments are confidential. Evaluatees cannot see who gave specific ratings or qualitative comments.
:::

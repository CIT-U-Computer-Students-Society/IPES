## General Questions

### What is IPES?
The **Individual Performance Evaluation System (IPES)** is a web-based evaluation platform created by the CIT-U Supreme Student Government (SSG) Committee on Research to digitize and automate peer performance evaluations across student organizations.

### Can I access IPES on my mobile device?
Yes! IPES is fully responsive. You can complete evaluations, review accomplishments, and inspect scorecards on any modern smartphone, tablet, or desktop browser.

## Officer & Member FAQs

### Why is my active evaluation queue empty?
If your dashboard shows 0 pending evaluations:
1. Verify that your administrator has transitioned the evaluation cycle to **Active** status. (Forms in *Draft* status are not visible to members).
2. Check if you are in the correct organization by clicking the organization switcher in the top navigation bar.
3. Confirm that your committee membership is currently active for the ongoing academic term.

### Can I edit an evaluation response after submitting it?
No. Once you confirm and submit a final evaluation, the responses are permanently locked to preserve evaluation integrity. If you made an accidental error, contact your administrator before the evaluation cycle closes.

### What should I do if my accomplishment submission is rejected?
If an accomplishment is marked as **Rejected**:
1. Click the rejected accomplishment in your portfolio table to view the administrator's rejection notes.
2. Address the feedback (for example, setting the Google Drive proof link to public or attaching missing documentation).
3. Update the proof link and description, then resubmit for review.

## Administrator FAQs

### How do I ensure only peers within the same committee evaluate each other?
In the **Assignment Rules** builder, select the **Peer-to-Peer** rule type and set the target to **Intra-Unit (Same Unit)**. Check the preview matrix to confirm that evaluator and evaluatee pairs are confined to their respective units.

### Can an administrator manually reassign an evaluation?
Yes. If an officer takes a leave of absence or a membership changes mid-cycle, administrators can add or deactivate specific assignment pairs through the Assignment Management interface.

### When should I transition a cycle from "Closed" to "Released"?
Transition to **Released** only after:
1. All pending evaluations for the cycle are either submitted or finalized.
2. All submitted accomplishment items in the review queue have been either verified or resolved.
3. You have reviewed the aggregate statistics. Once released, scorecards become immediately visible to all evaluated officers.

The **User Directory** (`/admin/users`) is where administrators manage officer memberships, assign students to specific units and positions, and grant administrative access.

---

## 1. Inviting or Adding Members

To register an officer into your organization, search for registered students by email or student ID, assign them to their unit and position, and select their organizational role:

1. **Select User & Unit**: Match the student to their designated committee.
2. **Assign Position**: Set their ranked role (Rank 1 for Chair/Head).
3. **Term Range**: Define active tenure (e.g. AY 2026-2027).
4. **Organization Role**:
   - **Member**: Standard officer access. Can complete peer evaluations, view personal scorecards, and submit accomplishment portfolios.
   - **Administrator**: Full administrative control over organization units, members, form cycles, verification, and audit logs.

:::caution[Automatic Assignment Signal]
When an active membership is created or reactivated while an evaluation cycle is in **Active** status, IPES automatically triggers assignment generation signals, ensuring the new officer immediately receives their relevant peer evaluation assignments.
:::

---

## 2. Managing Active and Inactive Memberships

In the member list table:
- **Edit**: Update an officer's position, committee, or administrative role.
- **Deactivate (Soft Delete)**: If an officer resigns or steps down, deactivate their membership. Deactivating preserves historical evaluation responses, ensuring that past evaluation analytics remain mathematically accurate.

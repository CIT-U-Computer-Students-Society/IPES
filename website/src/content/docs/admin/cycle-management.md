Once an evaluation cycle is active, administrators can track live completion progress and review participation rates on the **Cycle Management & Analytics** dashboard (`/admin/analytics`).

---

## 1. Tracking Real-Time Completion & Releasing Results

<img src="/images/annotated/admin/cycle-release.svg" alt="Cycle Management and Results Release Annotated Interface" class="guide-screenshot" />

### Action Sequence

1. <span class="step-badge-navy step-badge">1</span> **Live Participation Rate**:
   - Monitor the progress bar tracking total assignments, completed forms, and pending evaluators.

2. **Step 1: Close Submissions**:
   - When the deadline arrives, lock the form to transition status from **Active** to **Closed**. Once closed, members can no longer edit or submit responses.

3. <span class="step-badge-amber step-badge">2</span> **Step 2: Release Results to Members**:
   - After verifying all accomplishment reviews and final tallies, click **Release Results**.
   - The system computes weighted averages, radar vectors, percentiles, and publishes anonymized scorecards to evaluated officers.

The **Evaluation Form Builder** (`/admin/forms/builder` or `/admin/forms`) provides a flexible visual environment for creating evaluation cycles, defining custom competency categories, and crafting rating rubrics.

---

## 1. Creating and Authoring Forms

<img src="/IPES/images/annotated/admin/form-builder.svg" alt="Form Builder and Rubrics Annotated Interface" class="guide-screenshot" />

### Core Builder Steps

1. <span class="step-badge-amber step-badge">1</span> **Add Category**: Define a competency dimension (e.g. *Leadership & Strategic Vision*, *Execution & Technical Delivery*) and optional weighting percentage.
2. <span class="step-badge">2</span> **Rating Rubrics & Questions**:
   - Author 5-point Likert scale questions (1 = Unsatisfactory ... 5 = Outstanding).
   - Add open-ended qualitative prompts for constructive feedback.
3. <span class="step-badge">3</span> **Drag-and-Drop Reordering**:
   - Grab the drag handle icon (`::`) next to any question to reorder prompts effortlessly within or across categories.

:::tip[Draft Preview]
You can preview the form in Member View at any time while in `Draft` state to verify how evaluators will experience the questionnaire before activating the cycle.
:::
